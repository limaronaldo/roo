# Relationships first.

ROO — Relationship & Ownership Oriented design — begins with the obligations
behind an arrow: who retains data, who may change it, how long access lasts,
and what survives failure. Choose Java, Rust, or another language's mechanisms
after describing those decisions.

**v0.1.0: first edition (preview).** This edition uses the original seven-field
Relationship Contract.

Maintained by [Ronaldo Martins](https://github.com/limaronaldo).
Developed with AI assistance. External human review remains open.
