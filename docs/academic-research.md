# Academic research and proposed evaluation

ROO is a proposed practitioner method: an eight-field Relationship Contract
used in a six-step design and review procedure. The research question is
whether that integration helps people identify and address consequential
relationship obligations at an acceptable total cost. Neither the worksheet
nor the reference laboratories answer that question by themselves.

This development synthesis was prepared through **AI-assisted review**.
Separate model instances provide review roles, not independent human
validation. The [claims and limits](claims-and-limits.md#academic-position)
distinguish the proposed contribution from implementation evidence.

## What already existed

The [selective literature ledger](../research/literature.md) contains
**123 source entries**, including papers, books, theses, practitioner texts,
product documentation, and one unresolved thesis lead. An entry can group
several sources; 123 is neither a count of dissertations nor a claim that
123 complete works were critically read. Access notes distinguish metadata,
abstracts, selected passages, and full-text retrieval.

| Established antecedent | Consequence for ROO |
|---|---|
| Responsibility-Driven Design and CRC | Responsibilities, collaboration records, contracts, and scenario-based reasoning precede ROO. |
| Explicit relations, architectural connectors, and relational analysis | Interaction modeling and counterexample exploration precede the eight-field record. |
| DDD, ownership types, separation logic, typestate, and capabilities | These supply specific design practices or formal mechanisms. Their guarantees depend on their own models and assumptions. |
| Garcia and collaborators' agent/aspect work | Concern separation, interaction composition, and diffusion-based assessment are substantive antecedents. |
| Concept design and executable synchronizations | Compare identity, state, interaction rules, and scoped recovery mechanisms as well as the design procedure. |
| Specification planning, MAAD, ADD-guided assistance, and P/PeasyAI | Design assistance before implementation already exists. A documented workflow is distinct from an efficacy result. |

The selection constrains ROO's claims; it does not establish that no equivalent
method exists. The ingredients are grounded in prior work. Historical
distinctness and engineering benefit remain separate, open questions.

## What ROO proposes

The candidate contribution is a compact integration of **Custodian, Identity,
Coordinator, Authorization, Access, Horizon, Execution, and Failure**, coupled
to scenario selection, realization choices, counterexamples, and evidence.
The [canonical procedure](02-relationship-design.md#23-the-six-step-decision-procedure)
remains the method definition.

Keep four levels separate: asking about an obligation, expressing it in a
notation, enforcing it with a mechanism, and proving a property under stated
assumptions. A completed ROO record can elicit decisions; it does not acquire
the enforcement or proof guarantees of the sources it cites. No minimality,
completeness, independence of fields, or comparative efficacy result is claimed.

## Garcia, Lucena, and concern diffusion

The [focused comparison](../research/garcia-lucena-comparison.md) preserves the
complete chapter-8 source identity and printed-page localizers. The thesis
describes one Portalware system and four graduate students participating in
both versions, with the aspect version first. It explicitly rejects the
controlled-experiment label. The article's account of developer assignment
differs; that discrepancy remains unresolved. Seven selected change scenarios,
changed LOC as an effort proxy, and conflicting metric summaries require
qualified interpretation.

The original framework is jointly attributed to **Sant'Anna, Garcia, Chavez,
Lucena, and von Staa**. The [adaptation proposal](../research/roo-concern-diffusion-protocol.md)
defines CDC-R and CDO-R over independently specified obligations, logical
components, source operations, and direct references. It separates original
CDLOC shading/merging judgments from a proposed lexical transition diagnostic.
The paper/thesis CDO wording difference remains explicit.

These are unvalidated measurement adaptations. Removing a required permission
check can reduce diffusion counts while making behavior wrong. Structural
counts therefore accompany independent invariant outcomes, comprehension,
change effort, and total cost; they are not a weighted quality score.

The preserved 22-work thesis audit reports 15 selected-body readings, five
record/abstract checks, one catalog-only thesis check with a separate related
paper, and one unresolved citation. The [follow-up entries](../research/literature.md#follow-up-sources-for-the-22-work-thesis-table)
retain the source-specific limits. Selected-body reading does not mean a
complete dissertation audit or independent checking of its proofs.

## Assistance and the next evidence

The [upstream comparison](../research/upstream-design-comparison.md),
[source corrections](../research/upstream-landscape-review.md), and
[MAAD review](../research/maad-critical-review.md) distinguish author-reported
results, inspected artifacts, formal checks, and executed evaluations.
MAAD is a useful comparator; its reported mismatch-rate arithmetic and selected
repository inconsistencies require qualification rather than a superiority
claim for ROO.

The [assisted-design proposal](../research/assisted-design-protocol.md) maps
possible review roles to the existing method. The
[adoption analysis](../research/historical-paradigms-and-ai-revival.md)
treats training, stale records, false alarms, review, and maintenance as costs
to measure. Agent agreement and an executable test do not establish a correct
oracle or eliminate these costs.

The [revised research agenda](../research/agenda-revised-2026-10-12.md) calls for
traceable semantic comparisons, competent baselines, content/structure
ablations, independent requirement-derived oracles, assessor calibration,
equivalent resource budgets, and full cost accounting. Negative and
inconclusive outcomes must remain reportable.

The frozen MAAD pilot retains its own source inventory, cases, candidate keys,
budgets, and launch conditions. This documentation integration does not change
or execute it and supplies no new MAAD result. The earlier published agenda
and laboratory receipts retain their historical identities.
